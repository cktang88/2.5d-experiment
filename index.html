Build & craft

Press Q to choose a blueprint. Use Place Workbench, aim at supported ground and click. Press E nearby to open crafting; advanced recipes require a workbench. Tab or C opens crafting anywhere. R rotates; wheel or brackets adjust point-built structures. Right-click or Escape cancels points.

Defenses

Heavy weapons damage terrain and all structures, including your own castle. Place them with room for their blasts. Rocket batteries use four rockets per full volley; aim at a defense and press E to reload.

Other controls

Esc pauses · F1 or ? opens Help · B world map · N minimap · F5/F9 save/load · P pixel mode · O tilt shift · G ambient occlusion · V light shafts · M mute.
