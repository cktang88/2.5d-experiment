# Castle traps

Craft a trap at a workbench, select it in the build controls, and place it in a clear cell. Spike traps, arrow traps, flame traps and mines need a solid block underneath. Trapdoors need a solid block at the same height on one edge, so they can bridge a one-cell opening. Rotate directional traps with R; their front points along one of four cardinal directions. The placement preview shows whether the position is valid.

| Trap | Recipe | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spike trap | 3 wood, 2 metal | Damages hostiles touching its low, walk-through spikes, with a short cooldown per enemy. |
| Trapdoor | 4 wood, 1 metal | Press E while aiming nearby to open or close. The closed hatch is a full solid block; the open hatch is a real hole. Closing is blocked when an actor occupies the cell. |
| Arrow trap | 4 wood, 4 metal | Fires physical projectiles at visible hostiles in front. Holds 20 ammo; press E to refill from ammo in inventory. |
| Flame trap | 6 metal, 2 stone | Damages visible hostiles in front. Holds 30 fuel; press E to refill from goo in inventory. |
| Blast mine | 3 metal, 2 stone | Detonates once when a hostile steps on it, damaging visible hostiles nearby. |

Traps target enemies only and do not damage the player, wildlife, or blocks. Arrow and flame traps require clear line of sight; walls stop their attacks. Traps and their remaining ammunition are saved. Destroying a closed trapdoor or removing a trap's support removes its control. A trapdoor cannot close over an actor or a replacement block.
