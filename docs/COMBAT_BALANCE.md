# Combat balance

The defense picker displays each weapon's footprint, range, firing interval and damage from its current specification. Heavy weapons damage terrain and structures, including player-built structures and the castle; give their targets room. Actor health on the friendly side is protected from friendly blasts.

| Defense | Footprint | Range (m) | Interval | Damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Auto turret | 2×2 | 2–18 | 0.8 s | 45 |
| Heavy cannon | 3×3 | 8–32 | 3.5 s | 180 |
| Twin cannon | 4×3 | 8–38 | 2.8 s | 2×110 |
| Rocket battery | 3×4 | 12–64 | 6 s | 4×100 |
| Artillery | 4×4 | 18–90 | 7 s | 350 |

Minimum ranges are measured horizontally from the defense center. Explosive defenses hold a shot if its predicted impact is inside that minimum or its blast could reach their supporting footprint. Impacts that become unsafe after launch are neutralized. Explosions elsewhere can still damage terrain and structures.

The rocket battery launches four staggered rockets at a fixed aim point, then reloads for six seconds. A volley continues toward that point if its original target dies, and consumes one rocket per missile actually launched. Press E near a defense to fill its missing ammunition from inventory. The auto turret uses SMG rounds, rocket batteries use rockets, and the other defenses use shells.

The first four waves contain 30, 42, 54 and 66 hostiles; later waves grow to 135. The first wave comes from one compass front, the second from two, the third from three, and subsequent waves from four. The opening and between-wave build windows are 120 seconds. Starting early adds up to 50% to the reward on clearing the wave.
